AOrdering Requirements
  • Detailed Brief Required: Please start the conversation with a clear description of what you need, including reference images, layout sketches, or style examples. Vague greetings without details will be ignored
  • Scope Limits: I only accept small to medium-scale commissions. I do not accept open-world maps or massive cities
BPayment & Fees
  • Upfront Payment: A 50% upfront payment is required before starting
  • Final Payment: The remaining 50% must be paid before the final builds are transferred
  • Roblox Marketplace Tax: If paying via Robux (Gamepass/T-Shirt), you must cover the 30% Roblox fee
  • No Refunds: No refunds will be issued once the commission is completed/delivered
CProgress & Security
  • Anti-Snatch: To prevent asset theft, I will not share or grant access to the Place file until payment is complete.
  • Middleman: Using an official middleman is highly recommended for smooth transactions.
  • Deadlines: If I fail to complete the project by the agreed deadline due to my fault, a 20% discount will be applied to the final price.
DRevisions
  • Included Revisions: Up to 3 minor revisions (e.g., adjusting colors, moving small objects, scaling)
  • Major Changes: Any major changes to the original brief/layout after the blockout phase will incur an extra charge from 20% to 50%
EInactivity
  • Client Unresponsiveness: If you fail to respond to WIP updates or inquiries within 48 hours without prior notice, the commission will be closed. The upfront payment will not be refunded
  • Restarting Paused Projects: If a project is closed due to inactivity, a reactivation fee of up to 20% may apply to put it back in the queue (depending on the length of inactivity)
FCopyright & Usage Rights
  • Studio-Only Builds: All builds are created 100% inside Roblox Studio using Parts and Unions (no external 3D software). Please account for part-count optimization.
  • Showcase Rights: I reserve the right to post screenshots, videos, or showcase the completed build in my personal portfolio (Remind me if you do not want your builds in the showcase)
